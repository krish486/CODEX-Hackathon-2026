Organizer Overview

IEEE Student Branch - GCET

Established on February 28, 2006, the IEEE GCET Student Branch (Region 10, Gujarat Section) at G H Patel College of Engineering & Technology is dedicated to fostering technological innovation and excellence among engineering students.

Through hackathons, technical symposia, and workshops, IEEE GCET empowers students to translate theoretical knowledge into impactful engineering solutions.
